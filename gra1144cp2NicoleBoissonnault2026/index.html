Spicy Vodka Pasta

Spicy vodka pasta is a creamy, restaurant-style dish featuring al dente pasta tossed in a silky tomato and cream sauce enriched with a splash of vodka and a warming chili kick for heat. It’s indulgent yet simple, with a rich, velvety texture and a balanced flavor that’s both tangy and slightly spicy, making it a favorite for date nights, dinner parties, and cozy weeknight meals alike.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼cup tomato paste (3oz)
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon fresh cracked black pepper
  • ¼–½ teaspoon red pepper flakes (more if you like it spicy!)
  • 3 tablespoons vodka
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½cup parmesan cheese, freshly grated
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1lb mild Italian sausage
  • 8oz orecchiette pasta (or small shell pasta)
  • 1 cup starchy pasta water

Instructions

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the Italian sausage, breaking apart with a spatula until well-browned, about 5-7 minutes. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate.
  2. Boil salted water and cook pasta per package. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
  3. Cook shallot and garlic in olive oil until soft. Stir in tomato paste, seasonings, and red pepper flakes. Deglaze with vodka, then add cream, parmesan, and butter. Toss with pasta, sausage, and a little pasta water.
  4. Add pasta to serving bowls or plates and garnish with parmesan, cracked pepper and basil. Serve with a side salad or steamed or roasted veggies for a balanced meal.
